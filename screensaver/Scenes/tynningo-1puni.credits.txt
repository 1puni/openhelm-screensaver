Tynningö — Oxdjupet · OpenHelm open-data light study

A decorative chart and light study. NOT FOR NAVIGATION. The chart is an original,
simplified portrayal built only from openly licensed data; it omits features and is not an
official chart product of any hydrographic office.

THE LIGHT

Tynningö, Oxdjupet channel: Fl(2) WRG 6s, 5 m, nominal range W 6 / R 4 / G 3 M.
The rotating white sweep is an artistic interpretation of the light's period and sectors,
clipped at the first charted land. It does not reproduce the physical optic, brightness
or flash durations.
